University Management System

Integrated operations across the entire academic lifecycle — enrollment, courses, finance, and beyond.

1. Introduction

This document proposes the development and implementation of a University Management System (UMS) for the university's Business Administration program. The UMS is an integrated platform designed to digitize and streamline key administrative, academic, and operational processes.

By centralizing data and automating workflows, the system will enhance efficiency, improve communication, and provide valuable insights for decision-making across all university stakeholders.

2. Scope of Work: Modules Included

The UMS will be developed as a modular system, allowing for phased implementation and scalability. The following modules are included in the project scope:

🔧 Administration

  • Role-based User Management (Admin, Staff, Faculty, Student)
  • Department & Curriculum Management
  • Automated & Manual Timetable Scheduling

📘 Academic

  • Online Student Admissions & Enrollment
  • Attendance Tracking with SMS/Email Alerts
  • Exam Management, Hall Tickets, and Grading
  • Assignment & Project Workflow Tracking

💰 Financial

  • Fee Structure Design and Collection Tracking
  • Online Payments Integration
  • Scholarship & Financial Aid Management

📢 Communication & Engagement

  • Notice Board, Email & SMS Notifications
  • Student & Parent Dashboards (Timetable, Grades, Attendance, Fees)

📚 Library Management

  • Book Cataloguing & Digital Library Integration
  • Issue/Return Tracking & Fine Calculation

🏨 Hostel Management

  • Room Allotment System
  • Occupancy & Maintenance Tracking
