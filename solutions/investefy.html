Project Discovery Workshop: Key Requirements

This structured agenda ensures we align on the core vision, technical scope, and monetization strategy required for a successful MVP and subsequent phases.

  • What is the **Philosophy** and the ultimate **End Goal** for the customer using this advisory application?
  • What is the **Objective of the company** (Advisory for Investors) and the defined **process/workflow** for the end-user?
  • For **Logo Creation**, what is the desired visual tone (e.g., classic vs. modern, discreet vs. bold) and the preferred logo type (Wordmark, Monogram, or Combination Mark)?
  • Do we confirm the primary color direction of **Deep Blue/Navy** to establish trust, and what accent color is preferred?
  • What is the required **Number of Pages**? Are we targeting similar functionality to `smergers.com` but with a different design/UX?
  • **Mobile Target Platforms:** Are we targeting **iOS and Android**? What is the preferred technical approach (e.g., React Native/Flutter vs. PWA)?
  • Will the mobile app launch with **Full Feature Parity** with the web, or will it be a **Phased Mobile MVP** (which core modules first)?
  • What is the scope for **Mobile UX** elements like biometrics for quick login, offline reading, or background sync?
  • Are **Company related pages** (e.g., Careers, About Us) required, and who will supply the final content for these pages?
  • Define the **Required Fields and Media** per listing type (e.g., documents, images). What are the rules for **watermarks/redaction**?
  • What are the **Visibility Rules**? Which attributes are public vs. gated for verified/paid users?
  • Regarding the **Real Estate page**: What is the functional/visual goal, and who is the target audience (public vs. premium)?
  • Is **In-App Chat** the only required channel, or will we need Email Relay, voice/video, or other channels?
  • Outline the **Proposal Lifecycle**: required stages, **e-sign needs**, and audit trail requirements.
  • Is there a need for **AI** capabilities (e.g., enhanced matching, valuation tools, or a support chatbot)?
  • Define the **Monetization Model** (subscriptions, listing fees, commissions, or hybrid) and the specific features each tier **unlocks**.
  • What are the **Payment Gateway Preferences**? Are there requirements for **multi-currency** acceptance, proration, and chargeback handling?
  • Is the scope for **On-Platform Payouts** (marketplace escrow to sellers/advisors) needed in the MVP?
  • What is the required **Level of KYC/Vetting** for all user types (manual vs. automated checks)?
  • What are the initial **Jurisdictions** and applicable **privacy/compliance** and data retention requirements?
  • Who will be responsible for providing all necessary **Content for "How-To," Q&A, and the Blog** (if required)?
  • What are the required **Admin Roles and Permissions** (Moderator, Analyst, Finance, Super-Admin)?
  • What are the **Key Reports and KPIs** that the Admin Dashboard must track (e.g., MRR, registrations, match rate)?
  • Which **Third-party integrations** are preferred for Live Chat, Ticketing, CRM, and Email/SMS providers?
  • Is a **CMS (Content Management System)** needed to manage static content (FAQs, guides, etc.) without code deployment?

Timeline & Next Steps

  • Create a Phasing Plan (MVP vs. Phase 2) tied to acceptance criteria.
