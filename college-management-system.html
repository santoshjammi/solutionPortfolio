Actionable Intelligence: The Student Analytics Engine

The Student Analytics Dashboard consolidates over **80 performance indicators** per student from 10 key areas (academics, attendance, finance, engagement, and placements).

This powerful capability is driven by a **Hybrid Aggregation Model** (FastAPI, MongoDB, Celery), which computes denormalized data snapshots nightly. This approach ensures your dashboards are always fast, accurate, and provide **real-time visibility** for proactive management.
